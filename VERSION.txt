**Available Descriptors** (auto-detected):

- profile (side view face)
- sitting (horizontal elements detected)
- closeup (face > 25% of image)
- halfbody (face 10-25% of image)
- fullbody (face < 10% of image)
- outdoors (bright + blue/green tones)
- indoors (darker or neutral tones)
- neutral (fallback)
