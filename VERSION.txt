=== DETECTION TECHNOLOGY ===

**Multi-Signal Analysis:**
1. Smile cascade (3 sensitivity levels: subtle, clear, big)
2. Eye cascade + aspect ratio analysis
3. Mouth variance analysis (open vs closed)
4. Overall face variance (expressive vs calm)
5. Contrast analysis (lighting quality)
6. Gaze direction (eye position relative to face center)

**Scoring System:**
- Each signal contributes to expression score
- Highest scoring expression wins
- Additional descriptors added for extra detail
