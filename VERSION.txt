=== MAJOR FEATURE in v2.0.0 ===

✅ **LoRA-Training Naming System**: Industry-standard file naming!
   - Follows Kohya LoRA training best practices
   - 40% keyword only (melodija_001.png)
   - 40% keyword + class word (melodija_girl_002.png, melodija_child_003.png)
   - 20% keyword + descriptor (melodija_closeup_004.png, melodija_profile_005.png)
   
   **Class words**: girl, child, kid
   **Descriptors**: closeup, portrait, profile, neutral, halfbody
   
   **NEVER uses harmful words**: No fantasy, fairy, princess, magical, cinematic, dramatic
   - Ensures stable, realistic identity learning
   - Perfect balance for generalization without style bias
